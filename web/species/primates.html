Primates in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 91 cases involving primates from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-28, in 17 countries. 6 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Nycticebus spp., Macaca spp., Pongo spp., Hoolock spp., Pan spp., Gorilla spp., Sapajus spp., Callithrix spp.. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

384 import records of primates were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: specimen (scientific or museum) (135), skull (76), trophy (all the parts of one animal) (74), dead animal (whole animal) (20), medicinal part or product (15). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
