Orchids in the illegal wildlife trade

No news report of a orchids seizure has reached WildTrace yet. The trade is still recorded: countries report seized and confiscated shipments to CITES.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Orchidaceae, Cymbidium spp., Dendrobium spp., Paphiopedilum spp., Dactylorhiza spp., Orchis spp..

Where seized orchids came from, and where it was seized

807 shipments of orchids seized or confiscated were reported to CITES since 2015 (CITES Trade Database, source code I). Counts are shipment records, not quantities, and some countries report far more completely than others.

Seized at US ports

108 import records of orchids were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: medicinal part or product (91), live specimens (live animals or plants) (3), dried plants (3), cuttings (plant cuttings or divisions) (3), powder (2). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
