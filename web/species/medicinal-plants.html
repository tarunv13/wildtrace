Medicinal & aromatic plants in the illegal wildlife trade

No news report of a medicinal & aromatic plants seizure has reached WildTrace yet. The trade is still recorded: countries report seized and confiscated shipments to CITES.

CITES appendix I/II. Taxa covered: Saussurea costus, Nardostachys jatamansi, Panax quinquefolius, Prunus africana, Taxus wallichiana, Aquilaria, Rauvolfia serpentina.

Where seized medicinal & aromatic plants came from, and where it was seized

1,299 shipments of medicinal & aromatic plants seized or confiscated were reported to CITES since 2015 (CITES Trade Database, source code I). Counts are shipment records, not quantities, and some countries report far more completely than others.

Seized at US ports

134 import records of medicinal & aromatic plants were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: medicinal part or product (122), root (dead) (8), powder (2), unspecified (1), live specimens (live animals or plants) (1). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
