Parrots & songbirds in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 261 cases involving parrots & songbirds from public reports between 2024-01-19 and 2026-09-29, in 20 countries. 45 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Psittacula spp., Gracula religiosa, Psittacus erithacus, Ara spp., Amazona spp., Passeriformes. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.

Seized at US ports

1,195 import records of parrots & songbirds were seized by the US Fish and Wildlife Service (LEMIS; Marshall et al. 2025, Eskew et al. 2020; CC BY 4.0). Most often seized as: feathers (430), live specimens (live animals or plants) (365), dead animal (whole animal) (148), specimen (scientific or museum) (106), trophy (all the parts of one animal) (45). These records reflect US inspection effort and overlap the US seizures reported to CITES above: compare them, do not add them.
