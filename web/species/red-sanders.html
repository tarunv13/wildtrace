Red sanders in the illegal wildlife trade

WildTrace has recorded 100 cases involving red sanders from public reports between 2024-01-15 and 2026-09-28, in 2 countries. 2 of them rest on an official or validated source.

CITES appendix II. Taxa covered: Pterocarpus santalinus. Counts show where this trade is reported, not where it happens: reporting is uneven between countries and languages, and cases whose reports name no place are counted but cannot be mapped.
