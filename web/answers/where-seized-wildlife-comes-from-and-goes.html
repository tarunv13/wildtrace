Where does seized wildlife come from, and where is it caught?

Leaving the United States aside, the busiest seized route is India to China (333 shipments); US-bound routes are 83% of all 24,786 seized shipments reported to CITES.

What the number cannot tell you

Countries report seizures unevenly; the United States reports far more completely than most.
